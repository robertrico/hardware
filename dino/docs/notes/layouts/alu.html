Breakout strip — the pseudo card edge

A strip of its own, not columns stolen from the logic boards. Every signal that has to leave a board runs once from its chip to a numbered slot; the rig ribbon then plugs into the strip in one straight row and nothing reaches in among the chips. 17 of the 36 slots also relay a cross-board net, so the strip doubles as the backplane.
